WHO_IS_M_JIMMI?

I'm Dimitris Milios, a Flutter developer at SmartUp, where I build and ship production mobile apps for iOS and Android from a single codebase. On the web side I design and develop business websites and e-shops on WordPress and WooCommerce, with custom themes rather than off-the-shelf templates. After hours I'm an IoT enthusiast: I read, prototype and build my own connected hardware, from e-ink displays to lightweight navigation tools, and write the firmware and companion apps that make them talk.
